PixiePen

A place for kids to write stories, speak them aloud, and share them when a parent says yes.

Make a story

  • Write — Start a tale, save a draft, and come back to it later.
  • Speak — Dictate ideas with the microphone, then listen as the story is read aloud.
  • Illustrate — Add pictures from the photo library to a story or profile.
  • Collect — Turn stories into an e-book you can read in the app.

Share with a parent nearby

  • Children send stories for parent approval before they appear in the community.
  • Readers can like, comment, and rate stories that have been shared.
  • Badges celebrate writing, sharing, and coming back to the story world.
