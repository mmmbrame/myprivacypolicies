Support for Dune Smash

Help for Dune Smash, the physics-based football destruction game from Intellivex Studios.

About the Game

Line up your shot, set the power and curve, and kick the ball through ancient desert ruins. Smash walls, pillars, arches and crates, earn coins on every stage, and unlock special balls in the ball shop.

How to Play

  • Aim: Set your direction with the aim meter and trajectory guide.
  • Power & swing: Time the power meter and curved-swing meter for the best shot.
  • Wind: Watch the wind indicator; it affects the flight of the ball.
  • Special balls: Unlock Fire, Bomb, Rocket, Heavy, Spike, Golden and Multi balls using coins.
  • Goal: Reach the required destruction percentage to complete each stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection? The game is single-player and playable offline. An internet connection is only needed to load ads and process purchases.
Do I need to create an account? No. There is no account or sign-in. Progress is saved on your device.
How do I remove ads or restore a purchase? Use the Remove Ads option in the game menu. If you reinstall or change device, sign in to the same Google Play account and use the restore option, or contact us with your Google Play order number.
My progress or coins disappeared. Progress is stored locally, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. Contact us with your device model and Android version and we will do our best to help.
The game is slow or crashing. Close other apps to free memory, restart your device, and update to the latest version from Google Play. When you report an issue, include your device model, Android version and what you were doing when it happened.
How can I change my ad privacy choices? See the User Privacy Choices page.
