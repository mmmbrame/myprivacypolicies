Support for Rebuild With Me

Help for the educational disaster-recovery and community-rebuilding game Rebuild With Me.

About the Game

Rebuild With Me teaches players how communities recover after disasters. Travel through story levels, explore damaged and rebuilt environments, answer quiz questions, and complete mini-games—including first-aid activities, puzzles, and maze challenges—to progress through the campaign.

How to Play (Quick Overview)

  • Start from the main menu and choose a level you have unlocked.
  • Each level may include exploration scenes, a mini-game, a quiz, and a rebuilt area that shows recovery progress.
  • Complete activities to unlock the next level and continue the story.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rebuild With Me need an internet connection? No. The game is designed for offline play on your device. An internet connection is only needed to download or update the app from the Google Play Store.
Do I need to create an account? No. There is no sign-in or registration. Your progress is saved locally on your device.
How is my progress saved? Progress such as unlocked levels and quiz advancement is stored on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that progress may be lost unless you have a device backup.
Can I reset my progress? If your version includes a reset option in settings or the main menu, you can use it to start over. You can also clear the app's storage in your Android device settings.
The game is slow, frozen, or crashing. What should I try? Close and reopen the app, restart your device, make sure you have enough free storage, and install the latest version from the Google Play Store. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model and Android version.
I found a bug or have feedback. Email us at mmm.brame@gmail.com or leave a review on the Google Play Store. Please describe what happened and which level or mini-game you were playing.
Is the first-aid content real medical advice? No. Educational mini-games and quizzes are for learning purposes only and are not a substitute for professional medical training or emergency services. In a real emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
