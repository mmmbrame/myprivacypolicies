Privacy Policy for Waning Rift

Last updated: June 2026

Waning Rift ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile game application.

1. Overview

Waning Rift is a single-player, first-person exploration game set in ancient ruins. The game is designed to run primarily on your device. We do not operate online accounts, multiplayer services, or social features inside the app.

2. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device:

  • Gameplay preferences and progress: Settings such as audio or control preferences, and any saved game progress, may be stored locally on your device to improve your experience.
  • Device storage: The app may read and write small amounts of data to your device storage for normal game operation.

Information collected automatically:

  • Waning Rift does not require you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the app.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not require account registration or social sign-in.
  • We do not collect contact information through the app.
  • We do not operate online multiplayer or cloud save services for this game.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is stored locally on the device for gameplay.

4. How We Use Information

Any data stored on your device is used only to:

  • Run the game and remember your in-app settings or progress
  • Provide a stable offline gameplay experience

5. Third-Party Services

Waning Rift is built with the Unity game engine. Depending on your platform and device settings, Apple or Google may process limited technical information related to app distribution, updates, or crash reporting according to their own policies. We do not integrate Firebase, analytics, or advertising services in this app.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play / Android policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Retention

Gameplay data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from your device settings. We do not retain personal gameplay data on our own servers because the app does not transmit that information to us.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help you remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage on the device.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Play offline without sharing information with us
  • Delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings
  • Contact us with privacy questions using the support page linked below

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store listing for Waning Rift or the support page on this site.
