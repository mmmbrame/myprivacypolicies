User Privacy Choices for Dune Smash

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Dune Smash shows ads provided by Google AdMob. This page explains how you can control how ad-related data is used.

1. Consent Choices in the App

In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a Google consent form the first time you open it. You can review or change your choice at any time using the privacy options entry in the app's settings or menu, when it is available for your region.

2. Reset or Opt Out of Your Advertising ID

  • Open Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Choose Delete advertising ID or turn off ads personalization.

3. Manage Google Ad Settings

You can manage personalized ads on your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.

4. Remove Ads

You can remove ads in the game with the optional Remove Ads in-app purchase.

5. Delete Local Data

Game progress is stored only on your device. Clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove it.

6. Contact

Questions or requests: mmm.brame@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
