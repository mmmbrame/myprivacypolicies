Support for Aether Maze

Help for the action maze game Aether Maze.

About the Game

Navigate procedurally generated mazes, fight enemies, and stay ahead of collapsing paths. Aether Maze is a single-player action experience designed for offline play on your device.

Controls

  • Move: On-screen joystick or keyboard / gamepad movement controls
  • Look / camera: Drag on screen or use mouse / right stick
  • Attack / abilities: On-screen action buttons or mapped gamepad / keyboard keys
  • Jump / dodge: Jump or dodge buttons when available

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Aether Maze require an internet connection? No. The game is designed for offline single-player play on your device.
Do I need to create an account? No account or sign-in is required.
The game is running slowly or crashing. Close other apps to free memory, restart your device, and make sure you have installed the latest version from the store. Include your device model and OS version when reporting issues.
I found a bug or have feedback. Email us at mmm.brame@gmail.com or contact us through the store listing support channel. Please include your device model, OS version, and a short description of the issue.
