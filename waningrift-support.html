Support for Waning Rift

Help for the first-person ruins exploration game Waning Rift.

About the Game

Explore dark medieval ruins in first person. Move through maze-like environments, use your flashlight, and discover hidden areas as you progress through the world.

Controls

  • Move: On-screen joystick or keyboard/gamepad movement controls
  • Look: Drag on screen or use mouse / right stick
  • Jump: Jump button
  • Flashlight: Toggle flashlight when available

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Waning Rift require an internet connection? No. The game is designed for offline single-player play on your device.
Do I need to create an account? No account or sign-in is required.
The game is running slowly or crashing. Close other apps to free memory, restart your device, and make sure you have installed the latest version from the App Store. Include your device model and iOS version when reporting issues.
I found a bug or have feedback. Please contact us through the App Store listing review/support channel or the developer contact listed there.
