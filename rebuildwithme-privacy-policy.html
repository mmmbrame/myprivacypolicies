Privacy Policy for Rebuild With Me

Last updated: July 2026

Rebuild With Me ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our educational mobile game application.

1. Overview

Rebuild With Me is an offline educational game about disaster recovery and rebuilding communities. You explore story levels, complete quizzes, and play mini-games such as first-aid challenges, puzzles, and maze activities. The app does not require an account, sign-in, or online multiplayer.

2. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device:

  • Game progress: Data such as the highest level you have unlocked, quiz progress, and mini-game progress may be saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off.
  • App preferences: Settings you choose in the app may be stored locally on your device.
  • Device storage: The app reads and writes small amounts of data to your device storage for normal game operation.

Information collected automatically:

  • Rebuild With Me does not require you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the app.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services that identify you personally.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not collect payment information inside the app unless you make a purchase through the Google Play Store, which is handled by Google.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Gameplay data is not uploaded to our servers for normal use of the app.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information stored on your device is used only to:

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Remember your in-app settings
  • Provide a consistent gameplay experience

5. Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect information when you download or update the app, as described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Apart from distribution through Google Play, Rebuild With Me is designed to work offline and does not send your gameplay data to our servers.

6. Data Retention

Progress and settings stored on your device remain there until you uninstall the app, clear the app's storage, or use any in-app reset-progress option if available. We do not retain copies of that local data on our own servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Rebuild With Me is intended for educational use and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app stores only anonymous gameplay progress on the device. If you believe information was collected improperly, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete local app data by clearing storage for Rebuild With Me in your device settings or uninstalling the app
  • Reset in-game progress using any reset option provided in the app, where available
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at mmm.brame@gmail.com or through the support page linked from the Google Play Store listing for Rebuild With Me.
